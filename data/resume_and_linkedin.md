## 2. Resume — 3 Strong Bullet Points

> ✦ Built an end-to-end data analytics pipeline using **Python (Pandas, Seaborn)**, **SQLite (25 queries with CTEs and window functions)**, and **Power BI (19 DAX measures, 4-page dashboard)** to analyze 83,603 order records across 7 product categories and 5 Indian regions.

> ✦ Performed **RFM customer segmentation** on 5,000 customers, identifying 726 Champions (26% of revenue), 1,606 At Risk customers, and a Pareto effect where the top 20% of customers generate 41% of total revenue; recommended targeted win-back campaigns projected to recover ₹5–8 Crores.

> ✦ Discovered **₹31.1 Crore revenue at risk** from 9% cancellation and 6% return rates; identified 10 "volume trap" products with sales above ₹10 Lakhs but margins below 35%; quantified Q4 festive season concentration at 36.3% of annual revenue, enabling informed inventory and pricing strategy.
