Data-Driven Business Insights
⚠️ Electronics Concentration Risk
Electronics = 79.4% of revenue (₹162.6Cr). Single-category dependency creates supply chain risk.
→ Grow Clothing (45.5% margin) and Beauty (45.4%). Target Electronics below 65% in 2 years.
🚨 Groceries: 20.4% Margin Alert
Groceries generate only ₹1.6L profit on ₹7.9L sales — far below every other category.
→ Reduce SKUs. Stop marketing investment. Re-price staples upward by 5–8%.
📍 Central Region Underperformance
Central India = only 11% of revenue (₹22.5Cr) vs North's 25.3% (₹51.7Cr).
→ Invest in Central logistics. Campaigns in Bhopal, Indore, Raipur. COD incentives.
🎉 Q4 Festive = 36.3% of Annual Revenue
Oct–Dec drives ₹74.3Cr annually. Q4 2022 grew 108% QoQ. One bad campaign = missed target.
→ Build July–August mid-year sale. Begin inventory build 8 weeks before Diwali.
📱 UPI Leads · EMI is Untapped
UPI = 30% of orders. EMI = only 7.2% but likely the highest basket size (Electronics buyers).
→ Partner with banks for no-cost EMI on Electronics >₹20K. UPI cashback in lean months.
❌ ₹31.1 Cr Lost to Cancellations & Returns
Cancelled: ₹18.8Cr. Returned: ₹12.3Cr. Combined = 15.2% of total sales.
→ Video unboxing return policy for Electronics. Target cancellation rate below 5%.
🔄 Returning Customers: Volume Advantage
Returning AOV ₹40,900 ≈ New AOV ₹41,083 but Returning = 30,077 orders vs 19,923.
→ Loyalty programme: reward every 5th order. Re-engage customers inactive 90+ days.
🎯 1,606 At Risk Customers
RFM model identifies 1,606 customers who bought before but are now inactive.
→ Personalised win-back email with 10% discount. Auto-trigger at 60 days inactivity.
📉 Volume Trap Products
10 products have sales >₹10L but margins below 35% — mainly Home & Kitchen and Sports.
→ Reduce discounts by 5%. Bundle with high-margin items. De-list if below 25% for 2 quarters.
