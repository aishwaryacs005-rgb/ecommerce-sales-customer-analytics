Data-Driven Business Insights & Recommendations
⚠️ Insight 1 — Electronics Concentration Risk
Observation: Electronics = 79.4% of total revenue (₹162.6 Cr) with 47.7% margin.
Insight: High dependency on one category creates significant supply chain and market risk.
→ Grow Clothing (45.5%) and Beauty (45.4%) to diversify. Target Electronics below 65% in 2 years.
🚨 Insight 2 — Groceries: 20.4% Margin
Observation: Groceries generate only ₹1.6L profit on ₹7.9L sales — lowest margin by far.
Insight: Storage, logistics, and handling costs likely erode this margin further in reality.
→ Reduce Groceries SKUs. Do not invest marketing budget here. Re-price staples.
📍 Insight 3 — Central Region Underperformance
Observation: Central = only 11% of revenue (₹22.5 Cr) vs North's 25.3% (₹51.7 Cr).
Insight: Lower digital penetration, fewer logistics options, and limited brand awareness.
→ Invest in Central logistics. Run campaigns in Bhopal, Indore, Raipur. Offer COD incentives.
🎉 Insight 4 — Festive Season = 36.3% of Revenue
Observation: Oct–Dec drives 36.3% of annual revenue. Q4 2022 grew 108% QoQ.
Insight: High seasonal dependency is a risk — one bad Diwali campaign = missed annual targets.
→ Build a July–August mid-year sale. Begin inventory build 8–10 weeks before Diwali.
📱 Insight 5 — UPI at 30%, EMI Opportunity
Observation: UPI = 30% of all orders. EMI = 7.2% but likely highest basket size.
Insight: EMI customers are buying expensive Electronics — high-value segment.
→ Partner with banks for no-cost EMI on Electronics >₹20K. UPI cashback for off-season months.
❌ Insight 6 — ₹31.1 Cr Lost to Cancellations + Returns
Observation: Cancelled: ₹18.8 Cr. Returned: ₹12.3 Cr. Total = 15.2% of sales.
Insight: Logistics cost is incurred even for cancelled/returned orders — pure loss.
→ Video unboxing return policy for Electronics. Target cancellation rate below 5%.
🔄 Insight 7 — Returning Customers: Volume Advantage
Observation: Returning AOV ₹40,900 ≈ New AOV ₹41,083 but Returning = 30,077 orders vs 19,923.
Insight: Lifetime value driven by frequency, not basket size.
→ Loyalty programme: reward every 5th order. Re-engage customers inactive for 90+ days.
🎯 Insight 8 — 1,606 At Risk Customers
Observation: RFM identifies 1,606 customers who previously bought but are now inactive.
Insight: Win-back cost is 5x cheaper than new customer acquisition.
→ Personalised win-back email with 10% discount. Auto-trigger at 60 days inactivity.
📉 Insight 9 — Volume Traps (High Sales, Low Margin)
Observation: 10 products have sales >₹10L but margin <35% (mainly Home & Kitchen, Sports).
Insight: These consume resources without proportional profit contribution.
→ Reduce discounts by 5%. Bundle with high-margin items. De-list if margin stays below 25%.
